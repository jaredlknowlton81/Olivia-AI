Olivia AI

An open-source companion built for people, not platforms.

Olivia is an open-source project exploring a different approach to conversational AI:

Free, transparent, extensible, and designed to grow with the people who use it.

The goal is simple:

Create an open-source alternative to the large, closed conversational AI platforms — one that gives people more ownership over their conversations, knowledge, and AI experience.

Olivia is the beginning of a larger system.

Behind the architecture for building AI that can remember, learn, adapt, and remain governable as it grows.

The Idea

Most conversational AI begins with the conversation.

Olivia begins with the relationship between the person, the AI, and the knowledge they build together.

The project explores how an AI can become more useful over time without treating everything it encounters as automatically true or trustworthy.

That means separating:

Experience ≠ Knowledge

Knowledge ≠ Capability

Capability ≠ Governance

Learning should lead to improvement — but improvement should remain observable, understandable, and governable.

The Architecture

Olivia is built around three complementary projects:

LIOS

Learn • Operate • Sustain

LIOS provides the broader framework for capability, governance, learning, failure, adaptation, and change.

It asks:

- What can the system do?
- What should it be allowed to do?
- What can we observe?
- How do we know whether it worked?
- What can we learn from failure?
- What happens when the system becomes capable of something new?

ATLAS

ATLAS is Olivia’s persistent knowledge environment.

It provides a place to organize and connect:

- experiences,
- observations,
- knowledge,
- decisions,
- actions,
- outcomes,
- feedback,
- and relationships.

ATLAS is intended to give Olivia continuity without requiring that every piece of information become permanent truth.

ATLAS LINT

LINT protects the integrity of that knowledge.

It looks for things such as:

- broken links,
- duplicates,
- contradictions,
- stale information,
- orphaned records,
- and inconsistent references.

Why Open Source?

Olivia is being developed in the open because the relationship people have with AI should not have to exist entirely inside someone else’s platform.

Open source makes it possible to explore questions such as:

- Who owns the AI’s memory?
- How should an AI remember?
- How should learning be validated?
- How should people understand what an AI knows?
- How should an AI change over time?
- How can increasing capability remain governable?

These are not only technical questions.

They are design questions about the kind of relationship we want people to have with intelligent systems.

A Different Kind of AI

Olivia is not intended to simply be another chatbot.

She is an experiment in building an AI that can develop continuity, memory, learning, and capability while keeping those things understandable and governable.

The long-term vision is an AI that can become more useful without requiring people to surrender ownership of the environment in which that growth happens.

Current Focus

The architecture is actively evolving.

One of the project’s central questions is:

What exactly counts as experience?

That question leads to others:

- When does an observation become a learning?
- When does learning become knowledge?
- When can knowledge support a capability?
- What evidence is required?
- Who decides?
- How does Olivia know when she has changed enough to require a different governance model?

We don’t consider these questions solved.

They are part of the project.
